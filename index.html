About

I grew up in Uzbekistan, studied computer science at RIT, and now work as a software engineer in infrastructure and platform engineering in the Bay Area.

Most of my days go to Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS and data pipelines. Lately that work has been moving toward ML infrastructure, and I’m learning it in the open, from the point of view of an engineer who is new to ML.

Outside work I build products that solve my own problems. When I’m not at a keyboard, I’m usually lifting or riding my motorcycle.
